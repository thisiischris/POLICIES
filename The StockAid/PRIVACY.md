# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: June 2026_

StockAid does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

---

## What we store

User preferences (colour theme, header metrics, ticker watchlist) are saved **locally on your device** using encrypted storage (Expo SecureStore). This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

## Network requests

When you search for a ticker or load a company, the app fetches financial data from [Financial Modeling Prep](https://financialmodelingprep.com) via a Cloudflare Worker proxy. These requests contain only the stock symbol you queried — no user identifiers, account information, or device data are included or logged.

## Third-party services

- **Financial Modeling Prep** — data provider. Subject to their own [privacy policy](https://financialmodelingprep.com/privacy-policy).
- **Cloudflare** — proxy infrastructure. Subject to their own [privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## Analytics and tracking

None. StockAid contains no analytics SDKs, crash reporters, ad networks, or tracking of any kind.

## Children

StockAid is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from minors.

## Changes

If this policy changes materially, the updated date at the top of this document will reflect it.
