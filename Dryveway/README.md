# Dryveway

**Your garage, at a glance.**

Dryveway is the vehicle-maintenance app for anyone who's ever forgotten when the oil was last changed or missed a registration renewal. Add every car you own, log service in one tap, and let reminders tell you what's due — before it becomes a problem. No account, no subscription, no cloud: everything stays on your phone.

## What you can do

- **See your whole garage at a glance** — every vehicle with its photo, mileage, and a simple "all good" or "N things due" badge
- **Add a vehicle in seconds** — pick the make and model from a built-in list (or type your own), then add year, mileage, nickname, VIN, plate, and registration expiry
- **Track what matters for each car** — oil changes, tires, filters, registration, inspection, and more, each with its own status: OK, due soon, or overdue
- **Log maintenance with one tap** — confirm the mileage, pick a date, and optionally note the cost — done
- **Full service history** — every past service for a car, with dates, mileage, cost, and notes, plus a running total of what you've spent
- **Reminders that actually help** — get a heads-up before something is due, based on mileage or date, so you're never caught off guard
- **Make it your own** — hide items that don't apply to your car (no need for a cabin filter reminder on an EV), or add custom items for anything specific to your vehicle
- **Light or dark mode** — matches your phone or set it yourself
- **Add a photo** for each car so your garage is easy to recognize at a glance

Everything you enter stays on your device — no account required, no data sent anywhere.
