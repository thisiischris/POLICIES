# Staqqed — Your Cards, Finally Organized

**Staqqed** is the credit card wallet you've always wanted. Add all your cards in one place and instantly see what each one earns, what you owe, when payments are due, and how much your rewards and perks are actually worth. No account required. No internet needed. Your data never leaves your phone.

---

## What Makes Staqqed Different

Most people carry 3–6 credit cards and can barely remember which one earns the most at restaurants, when the annual fee hits, or how far along they are on a sign-up bonus. Staqqed keeps all of that in one clean, beautiful place — so you're always using the right card and never leaving rewards on the table.

---

## Features

### Your Whole Wallet at a Glance
- Swipeable card stack or scrollable list — your choice
- Search and filter by network, category, personal vs. business, or favorites
- Smooth, gesture-driven navigation that feels like a real wallet

### Everything You'd Ever Want to Track
- **Rewards** — see exactly what each card earns across dining, groceries, gas, travel, and more
- **Rotating categories** — never forget to activate or max out your quarterly 5% cash back
- **Sign-up bonus tracker** — know exactly how much you need to spend and by when
- **APR & promos** — track your regular APR alongside any 0% intro windows, with perks called out inline
- **Credit limits & balances** — monitor your utilization at a glance
- **Due dates & statement closing** — a built-in calendar so you never miss a payment
- **Annual fees** — see what each card costs you across the year
- **Perks & credits** — Uber Cash, travel credits, statement credits — track what you've used
- **Milestone rewards** — annual free night certificates, spend thresholds, holding benefits
- **Payoff calculator** — see your regular payment vs. a recommended plan that pays it off ~40% faster

### Portfolio Overview
- Total available credit, combined balance, and utilization across all your cards
- Side-by-side APR breakdown so you know where to pay first
- Expiring promos and grace period alerts

### Privacy Built In
- One-tap balance masking hides all numbers when you need discretion — reveal with Face ID / device biometrics, never a password stored anywhere
- Your card data is 100% local — no accounts, no cloud sync, no tracking of any kind

### Reminders (Lifetime)
- Local, on-device notifications for payment due dates, promo APR expiration, annual fees, rotating category resets, and sign-up bonus/milestone deadlines
- Nothing is scheduled through a server — reminders are generated and fired entirely on your phone

### Looks Great Too
- Light, dark, and system-adaptive themes
- Beautiful card art for 60+ popular cards built right in

### Bulk Import
- Already have your cards in a spreadsheet? Import them all at once from Excel or CSV

### Free vs. Lifetime
- Free tier tracks up to 3 cards, with full access to the core wallet experience
- **Staqqed Lifetime** is a one-time in-app purchase (no subscription) that unlocks: unlimited cards, export/backup of your data, the payoff calculator, promo/intro APR tracking, and premium reminders
- Purchases are processed via the App Store and managed with RevenueCat — see the Privacy Policy for what that involves
